# Fixed replies, used when the model is not consulted or its output is rejected.
#
# A missed call with no chat history gives the model nothing to personalise
# from, so it is skipped entirely - which used to mean the same sentence went
# out every single time. Anyone who called twice could tell. These say the same
# thing thirty ways instead, picked at random, never the same one twice in a row.
#
# One per line. Blank lines and #comments ignored. Do not sign them: SIGNOFF is
# appended separately, so a name here would appear twice.
#
# [casual] is used for everyone; [formal] replaces it for contacts whose saved
# name carries an honorific (Bhai, Sir, Dada, Didi, Apu...), matching what the
# model is told to do for those same contacts.

[casual]
sorry, couldn't pick up just now — i'll get back to you shortly.
hey, missed your call. i'll ring you back in a bit.
sorry, i was tied up and couldn't answer. talk soon.
couldn't get to my phone in time — i'll call you back.
sorry i missed that. give me a little while and i'll get back to you.
was away from my phone, sorry. i'll catch you shortly.
missed your call, sorry. i'll get back to you as soon as i can.
sorry, couldn't answer right then. i'll reach out soon.
hey, sorry — couldn't take that one. i'll call back.
was in the middle of something, sorry. i'll get back to you.
sorry i couldn't pick up. i'll ring you in a bit.
missed that one, sorry. will get back to you shortly.
couldn't answer just now, sorry. talk in a bit.
sorry, my phone was out of reach. i'll call you back soon.
hey, sorry i missed you. i'll come back to this shortly.
couldn't pick up, sorry about that. i'll be in touch soon.
sorry, was occupied. i'll get back to you in a little while.
missed your call — sorry. i'll get back to you soon.
couldn't take the call just now. i'll reach you shortly.
sorry, i wasn't near my phone. will call you back.

# Burst replies: sent after several unanswered messages, when the model fails.
# A different voice entirely - it speaks *about* you, not *as* you, and says
# outright that it is automated. That is deliberate: someone sending five
# messages in a row may be upset or dealing with something urgent, and a line
# pretending to be you would be worse than one admitting it is not.
#
# No casual/formal split here. A notice announcing itself as automated is
# already formal by nature, and slang would undercut the one thing it has to
# communicate clearly. {name} is replaced with YOUR_NAME.

[burst]
Hi, this is an automated reply — {name} is busy at the moment and hasn't seen your messages yet, but will get back to you soon.
